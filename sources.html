Reference air-quality stations

European Environment Agency (EEA), Air Quality Download Service

Used for
Hourly and daily NO₂, PM10 and PM2.5 at the 11 stations within 25 km of the centre; station metadata. Twin: winters 2024–25 and 2025–26, to calibrate the model and to validate it (leave one station out). Forecast tests: hourly NO₂ from Sep 2021 to Feb 2026. Verified data (E1a) up to Dec 2025; up-to-date data (E2a, preliminary) for Jan–Feb 2026, the verified value kept where both exist.
Licence
EEA content is published under CC BY unless otherwise indicated; re-use for commercial or non-commercial purposes is permitted free of charge provided the source is acknowledged.
Attribution
Air-quality data: European Environment Agency, Air Quality e-Reporting (verified E1a and up-to-date E2a), data reported by Italy.

Regional air-quality background

Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service (CAMS) European air-quality ensemble, served by Open-Meteo

Used for
Hourly NO₂, PM10 and PM2.5 on the CAMS 0.1° grid around Turin: the background term of the twin (b · CAMS), winters 2024–25 and 2025–26. Also at each station for the forecast baselines marked † (winter 2025–26).

Weather and mixing height

ERA5 reanalysis, Copernicus Climate Change Service (C3S) / ECMWF, served by Open-Meteo

Used for
Hourly 10 m wind, 2 m temperature, boundary-layer (mixing) height, cloud cover and shortwave radiation on the 0.25° grid: dispersion, the inversion lid and the stability class (winters 2024–25 and 2025–26). At the box centre, Sep–Feb 2021–2026, for the weather-regression baseline of the five-winter forecast test.

Emissions by sector

EDGAR v8.1 air pollutants, European Commission Joint Research Centre (JRC)

Used for
2022 NOx, PM2.5 and PM10 on 0.1° cells for road transport (TRO), energy for buildings (RCO), power, industry, non-road transport and totals. Road and heating emissions are downscaled to the 100 m grid.

Weather forecasts (leakage-free)

ECMWF IFS 0.25° open-data forecasts, served by the Open-Meteo Previous Runs API

Used for
For every hour, the forecast issued about 24 h earlier (_previous_day1): temperature, wind at 10 and 100 m, cloud cover and radiation at the box centre, Jul 2025 – Feb 2026. Covariates of the forecast backtest, so no future information leaks into a forecast.
Licence
ECMWF open data are licensed CC BY 4.0 under the ECMWF Terms of Use; Open-Meteo data are CC BY 4.0, its free API is for non-commercial use.
Attribution
Contains modified ECMWF open data (2025–2026). Weather data by Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0).

Forecasting model

TimesFM 2.5 (200M parameters, PyTorch), Google Research, via Hugging Face

Used for
Zero-shot forecasts of hourly NO₂ (next 24 h) and daily PM from each station's own history: the backtests, the five-winter test, the 2025–26 forecast-vs-reality test and the viewer's forecast tab. Not trained or fine-tuned on Turin.
Licence
Apache License 2.0 (weights and code). TimesFM 3.0 was not used: its weights are for non-commercial use only.
Attribution
Forecasts made with TimesFM 2.5 (Google Research, Apache-2.0). Das, Kong, Sen and Zhou, “A decoder-only foundation model for time-series forecasting”, ICML 2024.

Methods and standards cited

  • WHO Global Air Quality Guidelines (2021): 24-hour guidelines NO₂ 25, PM10 45, PM2.5 15 µg/m³.
  • EU Directive 2008/50/EC: data-capture rules (daily means need ≥ 18 valid hours).
  • Turner (1970) stability classes; Briggs (1973) urban dispersion coefficients as fitted by McElroy & Pooler.
  • Denby et al. (2020), uEMEP: sub-grid downscaling without double counting the regional model's emissions.
  • Chang & Hanna (2004): FB, NMSE, FAC2 model-evaluation statistics.
  • Das, Kong, Sen & Zhou (2024), ICML: TimesFM, a decoder-only foundation model for time-series forecasting.
  • Gneiting & Raftery (2007): proper scoring rules; CRPS from quantiles.
  • Diebold & Mariano (1995); Harvey, Leybourne & Newbold (1997); Newey & West (1987): forecast-comparison tests.
  • Künsch (1989): moving-block bootstrap for the skill intervals.
